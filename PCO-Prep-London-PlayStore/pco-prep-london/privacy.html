Privacy policy

PCO Prep London · Last updated 22 October 2026

Summary

PCO Prep London does not ask for, collect or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The app works offline once installed.

Information stored on your device

Your practice scores and the questions you got wrong are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage, so you can see your progress next time. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time with “Reset progress” on the Overview screen, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Links to other websites

The app links to official Transport for London pages. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for adults applying for a London private hire driver's licence. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not affiliated with TfL

PCO Prep London is an independent practice tool. It is not made by, endorsed by or connected to Transport for London.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
