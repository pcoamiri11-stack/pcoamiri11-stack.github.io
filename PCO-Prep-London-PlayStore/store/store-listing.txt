Full description (max 4,000 characters)
Getting your London private hire (PCO) licence? PCO Prep London helps you practise for both TfL assessments in one free app.

SERU PRACTICE
• 129 questions written from the PHV Driver's Handbook, across all 10 sections
• Instant feedback after every answer, with the handbook page to look up
• Multiple choice, complete-the-sentence and reading questions, like the real test
• A Revisit list of the questions you got wrong
• Key facts revision notes for every section

SERU MOCK TEST
• 37 questions in 45 minutes, mixed in the same way as the real assessment
• Marked against the 60% pass mark, with a breakdown by section

TOPOGRAPHICAL ROUTE PLANNING
• An exam-style screen: instructions, a Start drawing / Stop drawing toolbar, Undo, Clear, Brightness and Home
• Draw your route point to point on a street map in atlas colours, with one-way streets, no-entry signs, banned turns and road closures
• Short route, short route with a closure, and long route questions
• Your route is marked for start and finish, following the rules, and being the most direct, and you can compare it with a most direct route

TOPOGRAPHICAL MOCK
• 24 questions in 70 minutes: 3 route questions and 21 multiple-choice questions on map symbols, places, streets, road numbers and compass points
• Flag questions, review your answers, then submit for a score out of 100

LONDON KNOWLEDGE
• Quick rounds on main road numbers, well-known places and compass directions across London

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• No account, no adverts, no tracking
• Your progress is saved only on your phone
• Works offline once installed

PCO Prep London is an independent practice tool. It is not made by, endorsed by or connected to Transport for London. Always check the latest official TfL guidance before your assessments.
